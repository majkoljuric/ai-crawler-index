Methodology

Each tracked domain's robots.txt is fetched weekly. We parse the User-agent groups per RFC 9309 — the most specific matching group wins, so a bot with its own named block is judged on that block, not the wildcard * block. A domain is blocked for a bot on an exact Disallow: /, partial when some paths are disallowed but not the whole site, and allowed when neither applies. robots.txt is a public, voluntary signal, not an enforcement mechanism — it records what a site asked, not necessarily what every crawler obeyed.

Entries marked seed entry come from contemporaneous news reporting rather than a live fetch, and are kept clearly labeled rather than presented as scraped. Everything else is not yet checked until the scraper's first live run — that status is a gap in coverage, not a claim that the crawler is allowed.
